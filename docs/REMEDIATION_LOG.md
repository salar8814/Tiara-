## Business rules changed (and why)

These close states that the baseline left undefined. They are not silent changes to existing rules.

1. A quotation open when its RFQ is reopened or closed becomes `withdrawn` (new status). Before, it stayed approvable.
2. When the last open quotation expires, the RFQ goes back to `in_review`. Before, it stayed `quoted` with nothing to approve.
3. Approval requires an OTP-verified owner. Before, the code + phone cookie was enough.
4. Cancelling a paid order creates `refund_pending`. Before, there was no state for it.
5. Customers may cancel their own unpaid orders. Before, they had to wait for expiry.
6. Sessions last 14 days instead of 30.

## Not changed (needs a business decision)

These are listed in BASELINE_AUDIT §3:
- per-employee monthly purchase limits
- landline contacts' approval path
- partial and deposit payments
- refund execution
- company accounts
